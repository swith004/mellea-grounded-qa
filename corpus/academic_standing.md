# Undergraduate Academic Standing

Source: https://catalog.fiu.edu/policiesandprocesses/courses-credit/grading/academicstanding/undergraduate
Retrieved for benchmark preparation: 2026-11-10

This is a curated, paraphrased policy fact sheet based on the official FIU catalog page linked above; it is the controlled document corpus for this QA benchmark. It is not a verbatim copy of the university catalog. Use the official catalog for current policy decisions.

## Policy facts

- Undergraduates receive an academic warning when their cumulative FIU GPA drops below 2.0.

- An undergraduate already on warning is placed on academic probation if the cumulative GPA remains under 2.0.

- A student on probation with a term GPA of at least 2.0 stays on probation, rather than being dismissed, if the cumulative GPA is still below 2.0.

- An undergraduate already on probation is automatically dismissed when both the cumulative GPA and the semester GPA are below 2.0.

- FIU does not academically dismiss an undergraduate before the student has attempted at least 20 semester hours.

- Academic dismissal from FIU lasts at least one year.

- After academic dismissal, students have ten working days to submit an appeal for academic reinstatement.

- An undergraduate who is reinstated after academic dismissal returns on academic probation.
