## Policy facts

- FIU calculates its grade point averages from FIU-earned grades; grades from transfer courses are excluded.

- Term GPA considers courses taken in one term, while cumulative GPA reflects FIU coursework across the student's academic career.

- In FIU's grade table, a B+ corresponds to 3.330 points per credit hour.

- In FIU's grade table, an A- corresponds to 3.670 points per credit hour.

- The P grade carries no numerical grade-point value and is excluded from the GPA calculation.

- To compute GPA, multiply each graded course's credit hours by its grade-point value, add those products, and divide by the included credit hours.

- FIU rounds grade point averages to two decimal places.

- A regular incomplete grade may be awarded when no more than half the coursework is missing and the student is otherwise earning a passing grade.

- An incomplete must be completed within two consecutive terms, counting summer, or it automatically changes to an F.
