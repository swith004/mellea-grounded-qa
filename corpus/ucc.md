## Policy facts

- FIU's 2026 University Core Curriculum requires a minimum of 36 credits across its approved courses.

- The five UCC subject categories are Communication, Humanities, Mathematics, Natural Science, and Social Science.

- The Writing Intensive Requirement calls for six credit hours from two WIR-designated UCC courses, each completed with at least a C.

- FIU's UCC instructions specify that the approved core courses must all be taken for a letter grade.

- Students entering FIU with fewer than 30 semester hours must take the one-credit First-Year Experience course in their first or second year.

- For graduation, students complete at least one natural science lab associated with a Natural Science Group One or Group Two course.
