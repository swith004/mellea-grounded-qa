# Grade Forgiveness

Source: https://catalog.fiu.edu/policiesandprocesses/courses-credit/grading/gradeforgiveness
Retrieved for benchmark preparation: 2026-11-10

This is a curated, paraphrased policy fact sheet based on the official FIU catalog page linked above; it is the controlled document corpus for this QA benchmark. It is not a verbatim copy of the university catalog. Use the official catalog for current policy decisions.

## Policy facts

- Undergraduate grade forgiveness is available only for courses in which the student initially earned below a C.

- An FIU undergraduate can apply grade forgiveness no more than three times, regardless of how many bachelor's degrees are pursued.

- When grade forgiveness applies, FIU uses the grade from the final repeated attempt for the internal GPA calculation, although all attempts remain on the transcript.

- For grade forgiveness, both the original and repeated attempts must have been taken at FIU.

- A qualifying repeated course normally matches the original course's grading option and credit count; a credit-count change can justify an exception.

- After the bachelor's degree has been conferred, FIU will not apply grade forgiveness to that student's record.

- A student found responsible for academic misconduct in a course cannot use undergraduate grade forgiveness for that course.
