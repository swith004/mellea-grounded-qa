## Policy facts

- An FIU bachelor's degree requires at least 120 semester hours of acceptable coursework.

- For bachelor's degree residency, students ordinarily complete their final 30 credit hours at FIU, subject to advance-approved limited exceptions.

- At least 25 percent of the total credit hours required for a bachelor's degree must be awarded by FIU.

- Bachelor's degree candidates need a cumulative FIU GPA of at least 2.0, or a higher minimum if their program requires it.

- The listed foreign-language graduation requirement is 8 to 10 sequential credits in one language, and American Sign Language is acceptable.

- Students who enter FIU or another Florida State University System institution with fewer than 60 credits must earn at least nine credits across one or more SUS summer terms.

- Awarding two bachelor's degrees together requires at least 30 appropriate semester hours beyond the requirements of one degree.

- A student completing two majors concurrently receives one bachelor's degree, with both majors identified on the transcript.
