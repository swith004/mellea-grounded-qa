# Undergraduate Transfer Credit

Source: https://catalog.fiu.edu/policiesandprocesses/courses-credit/credit/undergraduate-transfer
Retrieved for benchmark preparation: 2026-11-10

This is a curated, paraphrased policy fact sheet based on the official FIU catalog page linked above; it is the controlled document corpus for this QA benchmark. It is not a verbatim copy of the university catalog. Use the official catalog for current policy decisions.

## Policy facts

- No more than 60 lower-division transfer credits may count toward an FIU degree.

- No more than 30 upper-division transfer credits may count toward an FIU degree.

- FIU may accept a transferred course graded D, but the grade does not necessarily satisfy requirements in the major.

- Academic departments determine transfer-course equivalencies that apply to their programs, including upper-division requirements.

- FIU students planning to take courses elsewhere for transfer must obtain advance documented approval from an academic advisor.

- Credit-by-exam cannot duplicate existing credit; when duplicate exam credit exists, FIU awards the exam yielding the most credit.

- Students can appeal transfer-credit acceptance or denial through FIU's Transfer and Transition Services office.
